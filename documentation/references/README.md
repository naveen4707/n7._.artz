# Loomis AI — Reference Materials & Visual Targets

This directory houses the foundational reference materials guiding the geometric construction, progressive stages, and graphite rendering aesthetics of Loomis AI.

## Reference Assets

1. **`original_portrait.jpg`** (Available):
   - High-resolution baseline reference portrait photograph (`reference.jpg`, Indian royal portrait in traditional saree and temple jewelry).
   - Serves as the primary validation asset for 3/4 head pose, complex hair updo, jewelry contours, and drapery folds.

2. **`loomis_reference.jpg`** (Provided in prompt):
   - Classical Andrew Loomis illustration from *Drawing the Head and Hands*: "How To Set up the Ball and Plane".
   - Illustrates:
     - The cranial ball and side plane slice.
     - Equator (brow line) and vertical centerline axis.
     - Jaw bucket-handle suspension and gonial angle.
     - Directional head planes tilted in 3D perspective.

3. **`progressive_portrait_guide.png`** (Provided in prompt):
   - 8-stage classical-to-digital progressive portrait guide (Robert Downey Jr. demonstration).
   - Sequence:
     1. Cranium (sphere with head tilt).
     2. Side Plane & Centerline (disc cut and facial meridian).
     3. Eye, Nose & Mouth Guides (horizontal thirds and keystone marks).
     4. Jaw and Chin (mandible attachment and chin wedge).
     5. Facial Features Placement (orbital sockets, nose wedge, lips, ears).
     6. Refine Eyes, Brows, Nose, Lips (anatomical planes and contours).
     7. Hair, Neck & Shoulders (silhouette mass, cervical cylinder, clavicle).
     8. Final Portrait (full tonal graphite modeling with visible underlying armature).

4. **`additional_reference_frames/`** (Folder created):
   - Dedicated folder for optional screenshots extracted from drawing-process videos.
   - Users or developers can drop video frames here to test specific lighting, angles, or styles.
